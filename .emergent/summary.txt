<product_requirements>
The initial ТИС КІС application aimed to be a comprehensive ERP system, covering core functionalities like User Authentication (with 2FA and RBAC), a Main Dashboard, and Basic Document Management. It was planned for expansion to include advanced features such as Enhanced Document Workflow, Calendar & Tasks, Analytics & Dashboards, and Improved Search & Filters. The long-term vision was a full ERP with modules like Financial Accounting, Management Accounting, Sales & CRM, Purchases & Supply, Warehouse & Logistics, Production, HR & Payroll, Document Management & Business Processes, Project Management, and Analytics & BI.

A new, comprehensive requirement emerged for a **Nova Poshta Logistics ERP**, structured into stages. Stage 1 focused on Corporate SSO, basic HR, and basic financial accounting. Stage 2 covered Operational Logistics (courier routing, tracking, fleet, warehouse management). Stage 3 encompassed Mobile Apps integration, and Stage 4 planned for Analytics & AI. The user explicitly requested full UI for HR (employees, departments, timesheets, business trips), Financial Accounting (chart of accounts, counterparties, journal entries, bank accounts), and dashboard enhancements (documents history, notifications, global search), all while supporting 50,000+ users. The system was rebranded to Nova KIC with Nova Poshta corporate colors. The latest requirement expands the system with detailed CRM (Leads, Deals, Products), Business Trip, Expenses, and Reporting modules.
</product_requirements>
